Ziraat Mobile Corporate

Time-Saving Transactions To Your Company At Ziraat Mobil

Ziraat Mobile Corporate Application is at your Service!

With our corporate account, you can quickly perform your corporate banking transactions by entering into our existing mobile application through the Corporate tab. With Ziraat Mobile Corporate Application;

  • You can perform banking transactions by logging into our existing mobile application.
  • You can log in to our enterprise mobile application with both main and sub-users.
  • With the main user and authorized sub-users, you can approve transactions via the 'Approval Management' menu and follow the approval status of the transaction sent for approval.
